# Epics

Each page below is one Jira **Epic** with its **Stories**. Open an epic to read, in this order: the epic name, its business benefit and scope, then every story with its business benefit, user story and numbered scenarios.

| Epic | Business benefit | Stories |
|---|---|---|
| [EP-01 · Access & navigation](EP-01-access-and-navigation.md) | Only identified staff with the right permissions can change customer-facing content, which closes the door to anonymous or unauthorised changes. One platform for every market and environment replaces separate country tools and cuts licence and support cost. | 9 |
| [EP-02 · Pages](EP-02-pages.md) | Pages are found, created and reorganised without broken links, lost work or accidental deletions, which protects customers from dead ends. Archive-before-delete and impact warnings remove a whole class of costly publishing incidents. | 8 |
| [EP-03 · Page builder](EP-03-page-builder.md) | Authors build on-brand, compliant pages themselves from approved components, without developers, which shortens time to market for every campaign and rate change. Built-in quality checks and reusable patterns catch errors early and keep every market looking like HSBC. | 20 |
| [EP-04 · Review workflow](EP-04-review-workflow.md) | Every page is checked by separate people for content and for business sign-off before it goes live, which builds the four-eyes control regulators expect into the tool itself. Clear hand-offs, tasks and reminders remove idle time between teams and make delays visible. | 26 |
| [EP-05 · Publishing](EP-05-publishing.md) | Only signed-off pages go live, together with every image and document they need, so customers never see half-finished or broken content. Scheduled releases go live at the agreed minute without out-of-hours work. | 6 |
| [EP-06 · Content library](EP-06-content-library.md) | Templates, assets, models and shared content are defined once and reused everywhere, so facts like rates are correct on every page and brand rules are applied by default. Accessibility data on every file supports inclusive design and regulatory accessibility duties. | 10 |
| [EP-07 · Translations](EP-07-translations.md) | Language versions are produced in minutes instead of being rebuilt by hand, so local markets launch at the same time as the original. Every translation goes through the same review, so speed never bypasses compliance. | 2 |
| [EP-08 · Governance](EP-08-governance.md) | Every change is recorded with who, what and when, giving the bank evidence for auditors and regulators on demand. A shared dashboard shows bottlenecks early, so managers act before deadlines are missed. | 3 |
| [EP-09 · Delivery API](EP-09-delivery-api.md) | Every website and app reads the same approved content from one API, so the bank stops duplicating content per channel. Clear contracts and schemas let development teams integrate quickly and with fewer defects. | 3 |
